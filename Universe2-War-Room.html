Our P7 plan

Our P7 game plan

Price Image like a leader, sell the Family cars we already have, and finally fix Eco's design.

Warning: MEGA's default P7 decisions are $1,059 over our cash. Our plan fits with about $330 to spare, and it uses realistic demand instead of MEGA's "sell every car" default.

Hold Low Cost exactly where it is, since it balanced perfectly in P6. Keep HR steady so the quality gains land. Start growing Image and Low Cost plants now so the capacity is ready for P8 and the merger.

Company-wide moves

  • 1Keep HR & Quality near $1,040. We just raised it, and the payoff (lower failure rate, more usable capacity, better morale) arrives this period. Cutting now would hurt morale.
  • 2Move plant money from Family to Low Cost and Image. Family gets $0 (shrinks 151 → 142, still far more than we sell). Low Cost +10 cars for $319 (the cheapest capacity in the game at $25 a car). Image +5 cars for $1,610. Eco stays the same size. See the Corporate budget tab.
  • 3Take the $2,000 loan. Debt goes to about 39% of equity, still at the 2% rate. The cash arrives in P8, when we'll want to grow Image and Low Cost plants for the merger.
  • 4No new Family capacity. Family is shrinking 5% a period, and Purple is sitting on 95 unsold cars.
